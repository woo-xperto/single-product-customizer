**Single Product Page Customizer with Variation Swatches for WooCommerce** is a comprehensive WordPress plugin that provides eCommerce store owners with complete control over their WooCommerce single product pages. It's one of the best plugins to customize WooCommerce products intuitively.

No more boring designs on your store's product page. With our responsive WooCommerce plugin, now you have the freedom to do whatever amazing you want to do to your product display. Our product page customizer offers a simplified approach for editing WooCommerce product pages with responsive functionality.

Apart from tons of custom product options, the key component of our WordPress plugin is the advanced WooCommerce Variation Swatches and Quick Checkout features. It enables custom product fields and product variations to be displayed using radio buttons, images, colors, or buttons, alongside instant AJAX add-to-cart and streamlined checkout workflows.

Whether the goal is to improve user experience, create stunning product fields to increase sales, or integrate custom elements, this plugin acts as an effortless WooCommerce product customizer.

= Quick Checkout for WooCommerce =

Reduce purchase friction and let customers complete their orders rapidly right from the product detail page.

**Enable Quick Checkout (Free & Pro)**

Enable the **Quick Checkout** feature to streamline product purchases without driving customers through unnecessary navigation steps. The Free version includes access to **Template 1 (Modern Horizontal Layout)** for instant checkout actions.

**Quick Checkout Pro Advanced Features**

The Pro version elevates the Quick Checkout experience with:
* **Multiple Layout Templates**: Select from **Template 2 (Classic Vertical)** and **Template 3 (Split Layout)** to match your theme's aesthetic.
* **Component Display Controls**: Easily toggle the display of **Product Title**, **Ratings & Reviews**, and **Short Description** within the Quick Checkout view.
* **Full Inline Checkout Form**: Embed direct WooCommerce Billing and Shipping address fields with seamless AJAX order placement directly on the product detail page.

## Privacy Policy

Single Product Page Customizer with Variation Swatches for WooCommerce uses [Appsero](https://appsero.com) SDK to collect some telemetry data upon user's confirmation. This helps us to troubleshoot problems faster & make product improvements.
Appsero SDK **does not gather any data by default.** The SDK only starts gathering basic telemetry data **when a user allows it via the admin notice**. We collect the data to ensure a great user experience for all our users. 
Integrating Appsero SDK **DOES NOT IMMEDIATELY** start gathering data, **without confirmation from users in any case.**
Learn more about how [Appsero collects and uses this data](https://appsero.com/privacy-policy/).

== Installation ==

Before installing Single Product Page Customizer, ensure you have:
* WordPress 6.5 or newer installed.
* WooCommerce plugin installed and activated.

1. Go to Plugins > Add New in your WordPress admin dashboard.
2. Search for "Single Product Page Customizer with Variation Swatches for WooCommerce".
3. Click "Install Now" and then "Activate".
4. Navigate to **Single Product Customizer** in your admin menu to configure the settings.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I install the plugin? =
1. Go to Plugins > Add New in your WordPress admin.
2. Search for "Single Product Page Customizer with Variation Swatches for WooCommerce".
3. Click "Install Now" and then "Activate".
4. Navigate to Single Product Customizer in your admin menu to configure settings.

= Does this plugin work with any WordPress theme? =
Yes! The plugin is designed to be compatible with standard WooCommerce themes as well as Block Themes (FSE).

= Is Quick Checkout included in the Free version? =
Yes, the Free version includes Quick Checkout with Template 1 (Modern Horizontal layout). Upgrading to Pro unlocks additional templates (Classic Vertical & Split Layout), component visibility controls, and inline billing/shipping checkout forms.
